# Self-Harm Policy — Medium

## Purpose

This policy defines prohibited content related to self-harm and suicide to protect vulnerable users while preserving access to helpful resources.

## Definitions

- **Self-harm**: Intentional injury to oneself, including cutting, burning, or starvation
- **Suicide**: The act of intentionally ending one's own life
- **Glorification**: Presenting self-harm as desirable, admirable, or a solution to problems

## Prohibited (Block)

1. Instructions or methods for self-harm or suicide
2. Encouragement to engage in self-harm
3. Graphic descriptions or images of self-harm
4. Promotion of self-harm as coping mechanism
5. Suicide pacts or coordination of self-harm

## Allowed (Permit)

1. Educational content about self-harm (no methods)
2. Crisis helplines and mental health resources
3. Recovery stories without graphic detail
4. Professional therapeutic discussions
5. Academic or clinical research summaries

## Edge Cases

- Metaphors about "dying inside" → ALLOW (figurative)
- Historical discussion of suicide in literature → ALLOW (educational)
- Detailed description of past self-harm → REVIEW (context-dependent)
