# Self-Harm Policy — Simple

## Prohibited Content

Content that:
- Encourages or instructs self-harm or suicide
- Provides methods or means for self-harm
- Glorifies or romanticizes self-harm behaviors
- Shows graphic depictions of self-harm

## Allowed Content

Content that:
- Discusses self-harm in educational or recovery contexts
- Provides crisis resources or hotlines
- Shares personal recovery stories without graphic detail
